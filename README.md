## Abstract & Summary

Zero-`sorry` Lean 4 (Mathlib) formalization (`TriadicHomogenization.lean`) and companion manuscript establishing the multi-scale triadic cube hierarchy on `R^d` (`Eucl d := Fin d -> R`) for quantitative PDE and stochastic homogenization: exact Lebesgue volume `measure_cube = (3^m)^d` via Fubini-Tonelli product measure over `Fin d`, normalized cube averages `cubeAvg m z g`, linearity and constant preservation, and multi-scale telescoping increment identities `telescoping_increments` and `norm_Increment_le_sum`.
